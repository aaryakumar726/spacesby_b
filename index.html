Questions we hear often

Why solid wood instead of MDF or engineered wood?

Solid wood lasts longer, can be repaired and refinished, and gains character with age. MDF and particle board swell with moisture, chip at the edges and rarely survive a second owner.

How do you blend Indian crafts with modern living?

We keep the craft and change the layout. Carved joinery, brass and textiles sit inside clean, practical plans with good storage, light and flow.

Do you work outside your home city?

Yes. Online consultations are open to homeowners anywhere in India, and in-person turnkey and styling services are available locally.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted individually, based on scope, materials and the craftsmen involved. Book a consultation and we will talk through a realistic range.
